User Data Deletion

Your Data. Your Privacy. Your Choice.

At Hotel Thar Heritage Resort, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. This page explains how you can request the deletion of personal information collected through our website, booking services or customer communication channels.

1. About User Data Deletion

We provide a process through which users can request deletion of their personal information held by Hotel Thar Heritage Resort, subject to applicable laws and legitimate business requirements.

2. What Information Can Be Deleted?

Depending on the nature of your request and applicable legal requirements, you may request deletion of information such as:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address and mobile number.
  • Website enquiry and contact form information.
  • Marketing communication preferences.
  • Non-essential customer profile information.
  • Other personal information that is no longer required for a lawful purpose.

3. How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your personal information, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to our official contact email address.
  2. Use the subject line: Request for User Data Deletion.
  3. Provide your registered name and email address or mobile number.
  4. Briefly describe the information you want deleted.
  5. Our team may contact you to verify your identity.

4. Identity Verification

To protect your privacy and prevent unauthorized deletion requests, we may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity before processing your request.

We will only request information reasonably necessary for verification. Please do not send passwords, PINs or complete payment card details.

5. Processing Time

We aim to acknowledge deletion requests within a reasonable period and process eligible requests within the time required by applicable law.

If additional verification or information is required, processing may take longer. We will communicate relevant updates through your registered contact details.

6. Information That May Need to Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained where required or permitted by applicable law or for legitimate purposes, including:

  • Hotel booking and guest registration records.
  • Tax, accounting and financial transaction records.
  • Information required for legal compliance.
  • Records necessary to resolve disputes or enforce legal rights.
  • Information subject to applicable statutory retention periods.

Where deletion is not legally permissible, we will retain only the information necessary for the relevant purpose and handle it according to applicable requirements.

7. Third-Party Services

If your information has been shared with third-party booking platforms, payment gateways, analytics providers or advertising services, you may also need to contact those providers directly to exercise deletion rights over information they independently control.

Their data handling practices are governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Marketing Communication

If you no longer wish to receive promotional emails or marketing communications from us, you can request to unsubscribe by contacting our team.

Opting out of marketing communications does not automatically delete booking or legally required transaction records.

9. Legal Compliance

We aim to handle personal data deletion requests in accordance with applicable Indian data protection laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and applicable rules and regulations, as they come into force and apply to our operations.

10. Updates to This Page

We may update this User Data Deletion Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, data practices or applicable legal requirements. Updated information will be published on this page.

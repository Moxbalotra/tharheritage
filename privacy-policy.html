Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters to Us

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

At Hotel Thar Heritage Resort, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your information when you visit our website, contact us or use our hotel services.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our website, contact our team or make a booking, we may collect certain personal and non-personal information, including:

  • Full name and contact details.
  • Email address and mobile number.
  • Booking details, check-in and check-out dates.
  • Number of guests and accommodation preferences.
  • Information voluntarily provided through contact forms.
  • Payment confirmation and transaction-related information.
  • IP address, browser type and device information.
  • Website usage data and browsing activity.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected may be used for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To process and manage hotel reservations.
  • To communicate booking confirmations and updates.
  • To respond to customer enquiries and requests.
  • To provide customer support and improve our services.
  • To process payments and maintain transaction records.
  • To improve our website performance and user experience.
  • To send promotional communications where permitted and with appropriate consent.
  • To comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve browsing experience, understand visitor behaviour and maintain website functionality.

Cookies may be used for:

  • Remembering website preferences.
  • Understanding website traffic and performance.
  • Measuring advertising effectiveness.
  • Improving website functionality and navigation.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

4. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics or similar analytics services to understand how visitors interact with our website. These services may collect information such as page visits, session duration, browser details and general usage patterns.

Google may process this information in accordance with its own privacy policies.

For more information, visit: Google Privacy Policy .

5. Google Advertising and Third-Party Services

Our website may use Google Ads and other advertising platforms to display relevant advertisements and measure advertising performance.

Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show advertisements based on previous website visits or other permitted signals.

Users can manage their Google advertising preferences at: Google Ads Settings .

6. Sharing of Personal Information

Hotel Thar Heritage Resort does not sell or rent personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

We may share necessary information with trusted service providers in limited circumstances, including:

  • Payment processing providers.
  • Hotel booking and reservation service providers.
  • Website hosting and technical service providers.
  • Analytics and advertising service providers.
  • Government authorities where legally required.

Such sharing is limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant service or legal purpose.

7. Payment Information

If online payments are available on our website, payments may be processed through third-party payment gateways. We do not intend to store complete debit card, credit card or banking credentials on our website.

Payment information is handled according to the privacy and security policies of the respective payment provider.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure or loss.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, provide our services, maintain business records and comply with applicable legal requirements.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally permissible.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Opt out of promotional communications.
  • Raise a privacy-related complaint or concern.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the contact details provided below.

11. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, social media platforms or third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of those external websites.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of third-party websites before sharing personal information.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website is not specifically directed towards children for the independent collection of personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization where required by applicable law.

13. Legal Compliance

We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable Indian laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and applicable rules and regulations, as they come into force and apply to our operations.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Hotel Thar Heritage Resort reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
